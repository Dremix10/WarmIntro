Leap Wordmark
01 · The leap as arc

The wordmark is the leap: a dotted arc rises from the start of the word and lands as a single terracotta dot above the final letter — the offer. Quiet, mature, recognizable. Best fit if you want one logo that does it all and reads "trustworthy mentor brand." My personal favorite for the YC submission.

02 · The funnel as islands

Seven islands rising in an arc — one per IB recruiting stage (networking → calls → coffee → referral → first round → superday → offer). The final island flies a terracotta flag. The Bridge
03 · Warm intro as bridge

Two small islands — the student on one, the bank on the other — joined by a dashed arc above. Most directly tells your warm intro is a bridge story. Strong narrative concept; slightly busier than the others, may feel cluttered at very small sizes.

Italic Wordmark
04 · The voice as the mark

Pure typography. The italic is the lean-forward leap motion, and matches the Fraunces italic mentor voice you already use everywhere. A single terracotta period — the destination, understated. Most adult, most "real company." Easiest to use everywhere. Risk: less ownable, more like a blog brand than a startup brand.

Trajectory
05 · Single-stroke leap

A single continuous arc from low-left to upper-right, gradient from Aegean to terracotta, ending in a filled landing dot with a soft halo. Pure abstraction of the leap. Most distinctive icon at small sizes; reads as "motion, momentum, arrival." Best fit if you want the brand to feel more builder than mentor.

My pick for the YC submission

Primary wordmark: #01 (Leap Wordmark) or #04 (Italic Wordmark). #01 carries more story; #04 feels more confident and grown-up. Both are safe.

Product / app icon: #06 (Monogram a.) — pairs cleanly with either wordmark and survives small sizes.

For the demo video / pitch deck: #02 (Archipelago Arc) is the most "ownable" mark and tells your IB-funnel story without a single word. Use it as a hero illustration even if it's not the official logo.

Avoid as primary: #03 (Bridge) and #05 (Trajectory) — both strong concepts but they read as generic SaaS at small sizes. Could work as illustrations inside the product, not as the logo.
